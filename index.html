Lublu

Artisan Bakery

Lublu is a small artisan bakery located in downtown Visalia, California that sells delicious pastries, sourdough, and other baked goods. With a name coming from the Ukrainian word meaning “I love”, our bakery places emphasis on community and quality goods.
