About Lublu

Who We Are

"Lublu is a small bakery and coffee shop in downtown Visalia. We make everything from scratch in our kitchen, with a big focus on croissants, danishes, sourdough bread, cookies, macarons, and other pastries. Our menu changes pretty often with the seasons, and we’re always working on something new. You’ll usually find a mix of classics along with new flavors and pastries we’re experimenting with. We also serve espresso drinks, matcha, and seasonal drinks, with a lot of our syrups and fillings made in-house. Lublu means “I love” in Ukrainian, and that’s really where the name came from. We wanted to create a bakery where we could make things we love and share them with our community."

Our Bakery

Be sure to visit our bakery during your walk down Main Street. We offer a cozy space for gathering that is dog-friendly, has plenty of indoor seating, and displays a variety of products from multiple local vendors.
